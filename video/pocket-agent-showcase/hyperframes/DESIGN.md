# Pocket Drift

## Style Prompt

Pocket Drift is a dark, cinematic product-film language for a local-first AI agent controller. A sparse black field carries a single electric-blue event trace between phone, daemon, and native agent. Interfaces are treated as illuminated glass surfaces inside a macro camera, with restrained parallax, deliberate focus pulls, and short technical annotations. The visual energy comes from depth and continuity, not from decorative gradients or fake telemetry.

## Colors

- `#000000` — base black
- `#151517` — primary card
- `#1F1F22` — elevated card
- `#4A9DFF` — PocketAgent blue
- `#32D74B` — connected / healthy state
- `#B584FF` — MCP / intelligence layer
- `#F2F2F4` — primary text
- `#98989F` — secondary text

## Typography

- `Barlow Condensed` 800–900 for short title cards and large labels.
- `IBM Plex Mono` 400–600 for technical annotations, event names, and feature labels.
- Display type is at least 60px; body copy is at least 20px; data labels are at least 16px.
- All stacked numeric labels use tabular numerals.

## Motion Rules

- Use a slow camera push for product meaning, a short whip-pan for topic changes, and a restrained orbit around the phone.
- Every scene has a clear entrance and one ambient motion layer.
- Scene transitions use a blue wipe / zoom-through language and never rely on an unexplained jump cut.
- All repeats are finite and deterministic.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use a full-canvas purple-blue gradient.
- Do not invent percentages, latency claims, or performance metrics.
- Do not use the five feature bars as a quantitative chart.
- Do not shrink UI text below video readability thresholds.
- Do not imply a cloud relay; PocketAgent is local-first.

## What This Layer Does Not Claim

The architecture and feature-pillar scenes are motion-graphic explanations grounded in the PocketAgent project context. They are not runtime telemetry. The five bars are a visual map of product capabilities, not a score or benchmark.
